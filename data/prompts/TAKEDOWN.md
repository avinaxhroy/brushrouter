# Takedown and corrections

The prompts in this directory are third-party text, credited per row in
[`ATTRIBUTION.md`](ATTRIBUTION.md). They reached us through the pinned upstream
corpus (`youart-open-source/awesome-gpt-image-2-5-prompts` at commit
`f7f6276`, plus the provenance records in `provenance/`).

If you authored a prompt here and want it corrected or removed, open a GitHub
issue on this repository and quote the slug from the ATTRIBUTION.md table — we
process those requests. You can also request it at the upstream source, which
propagates here on the next vendored refresh.
